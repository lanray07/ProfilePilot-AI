# ProfilePilot AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

ProfilePilot AI is a coaching and educational app for public sector applications, interview preparation, STAR examples, and career planning.

## Data Collection

The current app is designed to work with mock AI and local on-device storage by default. ProfilePilot AI does not require sign-in and does not collect personal data on a remote server in the current build.

Content that users enter into the app, such as job descriptions, application notes, STAR examples, voice transcripts, interview answers, application tracker entries, and coaching drafts, is stored locally on the user's device unless a future version clearly enables a remote AI or sync feature.

## Tracking

ProfilePilot AI does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

## Advertising

ProfilePilot AI does not include third-party advertising.

## Third-Party Services

The current build uses mock AI by default and does not require a third-party AI account. If future versions connect to a remote AI service or backend, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

## User Responsibility

ProfilePilot AI is an educational coaching tool. It does not guarantee job offers, interviews, promotions, or recruitment outcomes. Users are responsible for reviewing and finalising all application content before submission.
